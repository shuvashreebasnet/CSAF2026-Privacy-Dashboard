Who's Watching? Understanding App Permissions & Phone Privacy

This demo shows how everyday apps can access sensitive phone permissions such as location, microphone, and camera. Visitors will use a stimulated privacy board that displays which apps accessed certain permissions and whether that access makes sense based on app’s purpose. Through this interactive experience, visitors will learn how to recognize unnecessary permission access, make more informed privacy decisions, and better understand what information their apps may be accessing in the background.
